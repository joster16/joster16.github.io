# Is it really cheaper to get an EV in this day and age?

My current vehicle is a 2024 Ford Maverick that I've had since new. I've owned it since December 2023, and I'm just about to cross 75,000 miles with it. I do struggle to see how I've driven 25,000 miles a year on average, but back and forths add up. I recently had a friend purchase a 2022 Model 3 Performance, and has been raving about it. Not only about how it is in the performance category, its pretty wild there, but mainly how much cheaper the operating costs are compared to a gas burner. He said he was doing basically *$0.016/mile*, which is crazy, because running the calculation for my Maverick came out to be *$0.14/mile*. I had to do some analysis to back that crazy number up.
